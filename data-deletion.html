Gagrove Malawi Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 5 October 2026

If you want Gagrove Malawi to delete personal information that we control, you can submit a deletion request.

How to Request Deletion

Send a message to the Gagrove Malawi Facebook Page requesting deletion of your personal information. Please clearly state that your message is a data deletion request.

We will review the request and delete personal information that Gagrove Malawi controls, where applicable and where we are legally able to do so.

Information Needed

Please provide enough information for us to identify the information or interaction you want deleted. Do not send passwords, financial information or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Facebook Information

Information held directly by Facebook or Meta is controlled by Meta. Requests concerning information held by Facebook should be submitted through Facebook's own privacy and account controls.

Contact

For deletion requests, contact Gagrove Malawi through the Gagrove Malawi Facebook Page.
