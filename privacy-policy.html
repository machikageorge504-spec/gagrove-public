Gagrove Malawi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Gagrove Malawi provides news, vacancies and opportunities relating to Malawi. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with our services.

Information We Collect

Gagrove Malawi does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through its automated news publishing service. Our service may process information made available through Facebook, GitHub and other services necessary to operate the automation.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate, maintain, secure and improve the Gagrove Malawi service, including publishing news, vacancies and opportunities to our Facebook Page.

Facebook

When you interact with Gagrove Malawi on Facebook, Facebook may process your information according to Meta's own privacy policies. Gagrove Malawi does not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the Gagrove Malawi automation.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the service and limit access to authorized systems and accounts.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of personal information that Gagrove Malawi controls. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the Gagrove Malawi service owner through the contact information associated with the Gagrove Malawi Facebook Page.
